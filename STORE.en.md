# Google Play listing copy — English

The Japanese copy is in [STORE.md](STORE.md). Play keeps a separate listing per
language, so both are needed if the apps are sold outside Japan.

Lengths are within Play's limits: **title 30**, **short description 80**,
**full description 4000** characters.

**Do not reuse copy between the seven apps.** Seven listings that read alike
look like one app published seven times, which is what the spam policy is
looking for.

Points worth keeping in every listing:

- the app never uses the network
- the sound is computed, not sampled from recordings
- no ads, no in-app purchases

## 1. Akatsuki Synth

**Title**
```
Akatsuki Synth: Virtual Analog
```

**Short description**
```
An analog-style synth that computes its circuits. Sequence and export offline.
```

**Full description**
```
■ A synthesiser that calculates its sound instead of playing recordings

Akatsuki Synth models the behaviour of analog circuits as you play. There are no
sample libraries inside, so the waveform never breaks apart however high, low,
or long you hold a note.

■ What is in it

・Anti-aliased oscillators (saw, square, triangle, sine)
・A classic ladder filter, including the way it thickens as resonance rises
・Envelopes, LFOs, unison, glide
・A multi-track sequencer with a piano roll
・Export what you write as WAV, or as MIDI

■ It never goes online

Everything happens on your device. Every feature works in airplane mode. The app
makes no network requests at all, so what you play stays where you played it.

■ No ads, no in-app purchases

Nothing to buy after the app itself. Whatever you make with it is yours to
release and sell.
```
